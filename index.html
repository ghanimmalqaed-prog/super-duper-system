Coursework

Coursework 1# — Marketing Day

This course required us to develop our own business idea and bring it to life, allowing us to gain practical experience in marketing and selling products. As a team, we conducted market research to identify current trends and consumer preferences. We discovered a growing demand for personalized products that allow individuals to express their identity.

Based on this insight, we developed the idea of customizable mirror phone covers. These covers not only reflect the user's image but also provide an opportunity for personalization, such as adding names, quotes, or designs that represent the user's personality and style. Our product combines functionality with self-expression, making it both practical and meaningful for customers.

We procured inventory for a variety of phone models and collaborated with a third-party printing provider to produce customized designs based on customer orders. This process allowed us to efficiently deliver personalized products while maintaining flexibility in production.

Through this experience, I developed valuable skills in managing business relationships with external suppliers and gained hands-on experience using Adobe applications for design and customization. Additionally, this project enhanced my ability to understand and target specific customer segments, adapt to changing market trends, and respond effectively to diverse and sometimes demanding customer preferences.

Coursework 2# — NASMA

Nasma was a group project developed by my classmates and me, aimed at transforming the outdoor experience into a more comfortable and enjoyable one, particularly during the summer season.

The concept was inspired by outdoor malls in Qatar, where cooling systems are integrated beneath the flooring to regulate temperature. We proposed implementing a similar underground air-conditioning system at Al Liwan to enhance the overall shopping experience by making it more comfortable for visitors moving between stores.

For this project, we analyzed the layout of the location, identifying high-traffic areas to ensure optimal placement of the cooling system. We designed the pathway for the underground air-conditioning system, while also estimating labor costs, budgeting for equipment, and considering the legal requirements associated with the project. Additionally, we selected the appropriate AC units and developed a comprehensive budget plan.

Achievements

One of my key achievements was recognizing the potential of our marketing project and transforming it into a real business. Together with my co-founder, we continued developing the concept beyond the course requirements and actively promoted it in the market.

As demand grew, we expanded our production capacity and increased our operational workload to meet the needs of a functioning business. This experience strengthened my entrepreneurial mindset and enhanced my ability to scale operations, manage growth, and adapt to real-world business challenges.

Through consistent hard work, a strong focus on quality, and a commitment to meeting customer needs, we successfully established our brand as a trusted and highly recommended store, receiving positive reviews from satisfied customers.

As the business grew, the volume of daily orders increased beyond our capacity to manage effectively. As a result, we made the decision to temporarily pause operations due to the overwhelming demand. This experience highlighted both the success of our venture and the importance of scalability, resource planning, and sustainable growth in business operations.

In my pursuit of continuous learning and professional development, I attended multiple workshops led by marketing experts to expand my knowledge and skills. Through these experiences, I gained valuable industry insights and was awarded certifications that reflect my commitment to learning and self-improvement.
